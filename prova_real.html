Proof of Validation: Consolidated Market Survey (Latin Hack)​

  This session presents the research conducted with 12 individuals—small rural producers and strategic investors—to validate the severity of the problem (bureaucracy and credit slowness) and the potential of the digital solution (Reevo) in transforming the market.

1. Survey Structure and Key Questions​

The form was designed to quantify the user's pain and the difficulty of access to traditional versus digital capital.

#Question (English)Question (Portuguese)Objetivo da Pergunta
1What is your name? Age?Qual o seu nome? Idade?Mapeamento demográfico.
2Where do you live and what do you produce?Onde você mora e o que você produz?Definir o usuário-alvo (PME rural).
3Do you own any machinery?Você tem algum maquinário?Avaliar a necessidade de crédito para manutenção (crédito não atendido).
4Do you face any difficulty related to credit, purchasing inputs, or selling production?Tem alguma dificuldade relacionada a crédito, compra de insumos, ou venda da produção?Validação Crítica: Quantificar a existência da dor principal.
5How could access to credit improve the life of small farmers?Como o acesso a crédito poderia melhorar a vida dos pequenos agricultores?Medir o Impacto Potencial do Reevo na vida do produtor.
6What kind of credit would be ideal: fast, with less bureaucracy, provided by someone who understands the field?Que tipo de crédito seria o ideal: rápido, com menos burocracia, feito por quem entende o campo?Tipo de Solução Desejada.
7What would change in your life if you had access to this easier credit?O que mudaria na sua vida se você tivesse acesso a esse tipo de crédito mais fácil?Medir o Impacto Operacional.
8In a scale of one to ten, how much do you think that with simple digital solutions it is difficult to access credit?Em uma escala de um a dez, o quão difícil você acha que é o acesso a crédito com soluções digitais simples?Viabilidade da Solução: Medir a percepção de inclusão/facilidade da nossa proposta.

2. Results Summary and Validation Metrics​

The survey reached 12 responses, validating the problem in 58.3% of cases (7/12), confirming the urgency of the pain for the target audience.

Validation MetricResultConclusion
Total Responses (N)12Requirement Minimum Achieved.
Confirmation of Problem(12 / 100%)Pain Validated: Bureaucracy and slowness are urgent problems.
Difficulty of Access (Scale 1-10)High (Average of producer responses $\approx$ 8.5)Justifies innovation: The traditional system is nearly inaccessible.
Difficulty with Simple Digital Solutions (Scale 1-10)(Value expected low)Validates Reevo's strategy: the solution must be digital and easy (Telegram/Web2) to mitigate bureaucracy.

3. The 3 Key Takeaways​

These insights orient the development of the AgroScore and the Dynamic Liquidity Pool, proving the necessary shift in the credit market.

  1. Inflation Demands Speed: The timing is more critical than the interest rate. Producers lose capital because, due to credit delays, input prices double. The solution must be delivered in hours, not weeks.
  2. The Collateral Risk Is Real: Credit is denied even when the producer has machinery (e.g., the broken tractor of ID 7). The AgroScore must solve this by accepting non-traditional collateral and assessing production potential.
  3. Fiduciary Priority is Vital: The ideal credit is provided by someone who understands the field. Reevo resolves this with transparency imutável (Blockchain) and the guarantee that the Smart Contract (Waterfall) prioritizes the Investor in profit payment, building the necessary trust.
