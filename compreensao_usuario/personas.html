User Representations​

Sérgio – Family Farmer​


Source: Produced by the authors (2025).

Who is Sérgio?​

 Sérgio is 41 years old and a family farmer in the Midwest of Brazil. He manages a 32-hectare farm dedicated to planting corn and soy. He lives in a region with moderate-speed internet access, subject to fluctuations, but he uses his smartphone daily to manage contacts and research information related to production and the market.

Challenges​

 Sérgio finds great difficulty accessing bank credit. Bureaucracy, the requirement for collateral, and having a variable income tied to the agricultural cycle put him at a disadvantage compared to traditional financial institutions. Furthermore, he feels frustrated that his serious work and sustainable practices are not recognized.

Goals​

 He seeks faster, more transparent, and digital solutions to finance the purchase of inputs and small machinery. He wants to feel that he has greater control over his financial destiny, being rewarded for the good work he already does. For Sérgio, P2P credit represents a fair alternative, allowing him to grow with adequate rates and in a direct relationship with investors who believe in his potential.

Sérgio's User Stories​

info

User Stories are short, simple descriptions of a user's needs, written from the perspective of the solution's user.
They generally follow the format: "As a [persona], I want [action] so that [benefit]".
They help the team understand who the user is, what they need, and why, serving as a basis for prioritization, planning, and feature development in agile methodologies.


IDUser Story
US1As a farmer, I want to apply for credit digitally in a simple way, so I don't have to depend on bank bureaucracy.
US2As a farmer, I want to receive a quick credit analysis based on my agricultural data, so I can access money still within the planting window.
US3As a farmer, I want to finance the credit with fairer rates, lower than the bank or with greater benefits, so as not to compromise my entire profit margin.
US4As a farmer, I want to be able to prove my sustainable farming practices (e.g., certifications, reports), so I can obtain lower interest rates.
US5As a farmer, I want to track the status of my credit application in real time, so I have predictability and can make decisions about purchasing inputs.
US6As a farmer, I want to have a direct and simple communication channel with the platform, to ask questions about installment payments.
US7As a farmer, I want to have clear options of what to do if my application is not 100% funded on time, so I can make the best decision for my needs (accept partial, extend, or cancel).

Marina – Impact Investor​


Source: Produced by the authors (2025).

Who is Marina?​

 Marina is 35 years old, lives in São Paulo, and works in the IT sector. With an innovative and connected profile, she follows trends in technology, digital finance, and sustainability. She is familiar with digital investment platforms and is always looking for opportunities that combine financial return and positive impact.

Challenges​

 For Marina, the biggest challenge is finding alternative investments that offer real transparency regarding risks and that clearly demonstrate the socio-environmental impact generated. She realizes that many options in the market still lack reliable impact metrics or clear traceability mechanisms.

Goals​

 With a moderate risk profile, Marina seeks to diversify her portfolio beyond traditional fixed income. The ideal scenario for her is one that combines attractive profitability with positive and measurable impact. She values security, transparency provided by technologies like blockchain, and the possibility of choosing and monitoring the projects she invests in, seeing Reevo as an innovative solution.
