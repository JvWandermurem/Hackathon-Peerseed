Value Proposition

Value Delivery​

  Reevo's value proposition is delivered through an integrated set of products and services that use cutting-edge technology to debureaucratize rural credit and ensure capital transparency. The functionalities listed below are the core of the solution, demonstrating how the platform addresses the main pain points of the Farmer and the Investor throughout their journeys.

Products & Services​

Product/ServiceDetail/FunctionBenefit
Unified Digital P2P Platform (RF-CAD)Interface for Farmers and Investors, with separate but interconnected accounts.Centralizes credit origination and capital allocation in a single transparent ecosystem.
Automated Credit Analysis (AgroScore) (RF-AGR-002)Algorithm that processes multifactorial data to generate a numerical Score and risk classification more efficiently than other models.Resolves the slowness and lack of banking expertise.
Generation and Digital Signature of CPR (RF-AGR-003)Integrated service that automatically fills out the Rural Product Bill and allows qualified digital signature (ICP-Brasil standard), with biometric validation.Eliminates bureaucracy in loan formalization.
MarketplacePanel that lists investment opportunities, allows real-time monitoring of fundraising, and displays detailed data on the producer and risk (Score).Allows the investor to select the asset based on risk and impact, and the farmer to monitor their fundraising.
Integrated Secondary Market (RF-INV-003)Functionality that allows the sale of investment shares (tokens) between investors before the contract maturity date.Addresses the illiquidity of agribusiness investment, offering a way for the investor to realize profits or exit the operation early.

Gain Creators (Gains Generated)​

Gain CreatorDescription and Competitive Advantage
Record Decision SpeedThe AgroScore delivers credit approval in a much shorter time compared to the traditional market.
Mutual Transparency and TrustThe traceability of digital operations and detailed farm and risk data (including CAR) create trust and reduce the investor's perceived risk.
Payment Flexibility Adapted to Harvest CycleThe farmer can structure loan repayment with grace periods and deadlines aligned with their harvest cycle, which is rare in standardized credit lines.
Verifiable Impact InvestmentThe investor receives Impact Reports (RF-INV-007) on hectares financed and the supported producer profile, aligning financial returns with ESG values.
Liquidity and Portfolio ManagementThe Secondary Market functionality transforms a traditionally long-term asset into an instrument with liquidity potential, increasing investor portfolio control.

Pain Relievers (Pains Alleviated)​

Pain RelieverDescription of Pain Eliminated or Reduced
Simple and Guided Digital ProcessEliminates bureaucracy, paperwork, and the need for in-person visits or multiple forms, which are the main pain points for the farmer.
Didactic Risk ModelReduces the farmer's frustration of being rejected without explanation by the bank. The AgroScore provides clear feedback on factors that can be improved.
Integrated Tax ComplianceAlleviates the investor's pain of calculating and retaining taxes (IR/IOF), as the platform performs withholding at the source and provides the annual Income Statement (RF-TAX-002).
Automated Default ManagementReduces the investor's exposure to risk and losses, as the platform automatically applies penalties and late payment interest and notifies the parties (RF-AGR-006).
Access to Credit in BRL with Digital SecurityEliminates the technological barrier of digital asset investment, allowing the investor to use Pix without the need to manage complex wallet keys.
